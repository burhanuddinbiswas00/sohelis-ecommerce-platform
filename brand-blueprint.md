# Soheli's - Brand Blueprint & Strategy

## 1. Brand Concept & USP
- **Core Theme:** Fusion of traditional Nakshi Kantha stitches and rural cottage industry craft with modern minimalist and contemporary silhouettes.
- **Brand Tagline:** "Heritage Woven Modern" / "Tradition in Every Stitch".

## 2. Product Lineup
- **Saris:** Premium cotton, half-silk, or linen with exclusive Nakshi Kantha pallu and border embroidery. Soft pastel and earthy color palettes.
- **Two-Piece / Three-Piece Sets:** Chic cuts, minimalist embroidered kurtis/kameez with gorgeous hand-stitched dupattas.

## 3. Supply Chain & Production Setup
- In-house artisan team ensuring 100% quality control, perfect stitch finishing, and authentic handcrafted appeal.
