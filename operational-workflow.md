# Operational Workflow - Soheli's

1. **Fabric & Material Sourcing:** Procuring premium cotton, linen, and half-silk fabrics.
2. **Design & Sketching:** Drawing minimalist & traditional Nakshi Kantha motifs on fabrics.
3. **Hand-Stitching (Production):** Executed by the in-house artisan team of skilled women.
4. **Quality Check & Finishing:** Thread cutting, dry cleaning/ironing, and premium eco-friendly packaging (craft boxes, tissue paper, branded tags, and handwritten thank-you notes).
5. **Content Shoot:** Flat-lay photography and behind-the-scenes (BTS) reels of artisans at work.
6. **Social Media & Order Management:** Direct customer acquisition via Facebook Page, Instagram, and website.
7. **Delivery & Feedback:** Fast and secure courier delivery (RedX, Pathao, Steadfast) with customer feedback loops.
